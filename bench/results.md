# SELF benchmarks

Generated by `bench/run.sh`. Host: `Linux 7.0.10 x86_64`.
hyperfine -N warmup=30 min-runs=300; times are mean wall-clock per exec.

| subject | ELF | SELF memfd (M1) | SELF native (M2) | memfd/ELF | native/ELF |

## Notes

- **memfd (M1)** pays for reconstructing the ELF into a memfd plus a
  second execve; it is the honest worst case and still a small
  constant over a bare exec.
- **native (M2)** maps segments directly and hands off to ld.so,
  avoiding the second execve; it trades that for doing ld.so's
  bookkeeping in userspace.
- Neither mode shares text pages across processes the way a normal
  ELF mapping does (DESIGN §8, §9): the segment blobs are copied, not
  mapped, because SQLite overflow pages are not page-aligned.
- SELF file size carries SQLite's b-tree overhead but makes stripping
  a transaction (`DELETE`+`VACUUM`) rather than offset surgery.
